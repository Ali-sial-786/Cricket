CREASE VR

Original cricket for Meta Quest 3 / 3S. Hold the right controller like a bat handle.

Desktop debug mode (no headset):
Batting — move the mouse up to lift the bat, flick down to swing; left/right aims the swing (off / leg side). Space next ball · Esc/M menu (click to select) · C camera · T ball trail.
Bowling — hold the left button, sweep the mouse top→bottom (overarm) and release as the arm passes vertical (mouse near mid-screen). Q/E wrist roll (off/leg spin) · Z outswing · X inswing · ←↑↓→ move aim marker · B auto-bowl at the marker.
