In the headset

  • BatRight controller. Swing for real; the face angle lifts or keeps it down.
  • RunX run · Y go back · left trigger to dive.
  • BowlHold grip to run in, hold trigger, bowl overarm and let go.
  • MenuClick the left stick. Point and pull the trigger to select.

Desktop debug mode

Batting — move the mouse up to lift the bat, flick down to swing; left/right aims (off / leg side). Space next ball · Esc/M menu (click to select) · C camera · T ball trail.

Bowling — 1–4 bowler · Z/X next / previous delivery · ←↑↓→ move the target · hold R to run in, then hold the left button, sweep the mouse top→bottom (overarm) and release near mid-screen · B runs in and bowls by itself.

Clear a 2.5 × 2.5 m space before you play. Everything here is original and fictional.
