## 4. Root causes found (do not rediscover)
- **Dive hangs in the air ~6 s (1:24)**: `fieldController` did `if (r.running) r.p = r.target` when the throw arrived and the batter was judged home ("just home"). `Runs.update` only clears `diving` inside `if (this.running)`, so `diving` stayed true forever with `diveT` frozen mid-flight → `state().dive` frozen → flat pose held until the next ball's `reset()`. It also teleported him to the line. Now `Runs.makeGround()` marks the run safe and lets the slide finish.
- **Body twists in the run-out replay (1:35.5)**: same teleport put `p` on an integer, `state()` then takes the NEXT leg's direction → heading flips 180° at 9 rad/s while lying flat.
- **Hover in flight**: old pose put the stretched arms/bat below the turf; `groundGuard` lifted the whole body up to 0.38 m.
- **Stale dive/other pose on reappearing figures**: `Actor.smooth()` kept `_raw/_shown/_from` from the last time the figure was posed; a hidden figure shown again cross-faded from that old pose (runner in your kit; also AI striker bat→run, since `batProcedural` never calls `smooth`). The horizontal batter at the start of replay 6 (2:34.7) is `playerBody` (the batting worker's solve / `PlayerBodyView.resolve`), not a dive state — not fixed here.
- **AI striker snap-turn after his shot (5:42.4)**: `fieldController` set `batterView.running = r.state(true)` from the frame of the hit (runs active), so the shot's follow-through was replaced by an idle stand facing down the pitch within 0.18 s.
- **Blue #73 walking at you, standing in front, vanishing at the run-up (2:21–2:25)**: your runner (`batterView.actor`, `runnerOnly`). After a boundary `Runs.close({boundary})` walks him back to your crease; `state()` heading when stopped is the leg direction (back to you); he stood there until `clearRunners()` → `show(false)` in `prepare()`.
- **Two batters in front after a run-out (3:01)**: `close({stop:true})` leaves both where they are until the next `reset()` teleports/hides them.
- **Teleports / instant visibility (all the places)**: `FieldController.clearRunners` (`nonStrikerView.reset()` → `place`, runner `show(false)`, `swapBatters`), `NonStrikerView.setSide` (`place`), `UmpireView.place` on side change, `BowlerActor.startRunup` (`pos.set` to the mark from wherever he was), bowler figure ⇄ fielding-bowler figure swap at different places (`figures.bowler.show(bf.hidden)`, incl. the receiver going `state='set'` = hidden while taking the throw), `Fielding.newBall` (`far > 40` → `pos.copy(home)`), `Fielding.setSpinner` (keeper/slip `pos.copy(home)`), runner hidden within 1.5 m of your head (`RUNNER_HIDE`), `playerBody` hidden while the runner is out, keeper/fielder dive lunge offset dropped in one frame at the end of the dive, `separate()` hard pushes (up to 1.7 m when a new obstacle appears), catch fielder un-hidden at your head when the catch ends but the view has not returned.
- **Blocks in the striker's sight line during the run-up (58 incidents / 217 balls at HEAD)**: all were the fielding-bowler figure (`f-bowler`, state `return`) drawn as a second bowler standing/jogging on the bowler's line while the real bowler figure ran in (newBall set him to `return` → visible).
- **Blue #37 "pops in" 1 m in front of the bowler and stays 8 s (5:45)**: when you bowl, a fielder is sent to the bowler's-end stumps to take throws (`receiverSpot('bowling')` = 0.6 m behind the stumps, i.e. in your space). Fielders only started returning at `field.reset()` (end of settle); at that moment `toMark()` moves YOU back to your mark (view cut) right behind him, and the "Choose your bowler" menu pauses the game, freezing him there. Same fielder is the 5:39.16 "appears beside the player" (running to those stumps).
